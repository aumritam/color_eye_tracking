Mouse test mode is active. Hover over the canvas to test color naming.

To use eye tracking, click Start eye tracking, allow camera access, and complete calibration. After calibration, look at the canvas without moving your mouse.

Instructions

  1. Test the colors: Move your mouse over the palette.
  2. Start eye tracking: Click the blue button and allow camera access.
  3. Calibrate: Look directly at each red dot and click it five times. Keep your head relatively still.
  4. Test your gaze: Once calibration finishes, look around the canvas without moving your mouse.
  5. Switch or stop: Use Back to mouse tracking to compare against your cursor (the same button switches back to your eyes), or Stop eye tracking to pause. Resuming keeps your calibration.

Webcam access requires HTTPS or a supported localhost address. Eye-tracking accuracy depends on lighting, camera position, and calibration.
